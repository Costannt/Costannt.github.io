反馈连接的状态空间表达式

学校最近在讲现代控制理论，使用刘豹老师的教材。其中关于反馈连接状态空间表达式的推导语焉不详，遂自行推导。

考察初始状态空间表达式：

{X1˙=A1X1+B1u1Y1=C1X1+D1u1X2˙=A2X2+B2u2Y2=C2X2+D2u2\left\{\begin{matrix} \dot{X_1 }=A_1 X_1+B_1u_1\\ Y_1=C_1X_1+D_1u_1\\ \dot{X_2 }=A_2 X_2+B_2u_2\\ Y_2=C_2X_2+D_2u_2\\ \end{matrix}\right. ⎩⎨⎧​X1​˙​=A1​X1​+B1​u1​Y1​=C1​X1​+D1​u1​X2​˙​=A2​X2​+B2​u2​Y2​=C2​X2​+D2​u2​​

其中：

{u1=u−Y2u2=Y1\begin{align} &\left\{ \begin{aligned} &u_1 = u - Y_2 \\ &u_2 = Y_1 \end{aligned} \right. \end{align} ​{​u1​=u−Y2​u2​=Y1​​​​

将其代入立刻有:

{X1˙=A1X1+B1(u−Y2)①Y1=C1X1+D1(u−Y2)②X2˙=A2X2+B2Y1③Y2=C2X2+D2Y1④\begin{align} &\left\{ \begin{aligned} &\dot{X_1 }=A_1 X_1+B_1(u-Y_2) \quad &①\\ &Y_1=C_1X_1+D_1(u-Y_2)\quad &②\\ &\dot{X_2 }=A_2 X_2+B_2Y_1\quad &③\\ &Y_2=C_2X_2+D_2Y_1\quad &④\\ \end{aligned} \right. \end{align} ​⎩⎨⎧​​X1​˙​=A1​X1​+B1​(u−Y2​)Y1​=C1​X1​+D1​(u−Y2​)X2​˙​=A2​X2​+B2​Y1​Y2​=C2​X2​+D2​Y1​​①②③④​​​

联立②,④:

Y1=C1X1+D1(u−C2X2−D2Y1)=C1X1−D1C2X2−D1D2Y1+D1u\begin{align}Y_1&=C_1X_1+D_1(u-C_2X_2-D_2Y_1)\\ &=C_1X_1-D_1C_2X_2-D_1D_2Y_1+D_1u \\ \end{align} Y1​​=C1​X1​+D1​(u−C2​X2​−D2​Y1​)=C1​X1​−D1​C2​X2​−D1​D2​Y1​+D1​u​​

⇒(I+D1D2)Y1=C1X1−D1C2X2+D1u⇒Y1=(I+D1D2)−1C1X1−(I+D1D2)−1D1C2X2+(I+D1D2)−1D1u(1)\Rightarrow (I+D_1D_2)Y_1=C_1X_1-D_1C_2X_2+D_1u \\ \Rightarrow Y_1=(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}C_1X_1-(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1C_2X_2+(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1u \tag{1} ⇒(I+D1​D2​)Y1​=C1​X1​−D1​C2​X2​+D1​u⇒Y1​=(I+D1​D2​)−1C1​X1​−(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​C2​X2​+(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​u(1)

将(1)代入④:

Y2=C2X2+D2Y1=C2X2+D2[(I+D1D2)−1C1X1−(I+D1D2)−1D1C2X2+(I+D1D2)−1D1u]=D2(I+D1D2)−1C1X1+[C2−D2(I+D1D2)−1D1C2]X2+D2(I+D1D2)−1D1u\begin{align} Y_2&=C_2X_2+D_2Y_1\\ &=C_2X_2+D_2[(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}C_1X_1-(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1C_2X_2+(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1u]\\ &=D_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}C_1X_1+[C_2-D_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1C_2]X_2+D_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1u \tag{2} \end{align} Y2​​=C2​X2​+D2​Y1​=C2​X2​+D2​[(I+D1​D2​)−1C1​X1​−(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​C2​X2​+(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​u]=D2​(I+D1​D2​)−1C1​X1​+[C2​−D2​(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​C2​]X2​+D2​(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​u​(2)​

将(2)代入①:

X1˙=A1X1+B1(u−Y2)=A1X1+B1u−B1{D2(I+D1D2)−1C1X1+[C2−D2(I+D1D2)−1D1C2]X2+D2(I+D1D2)−1D1u}=[A1−B1D2(I+D1D2)−1C1]X1+[B1D2(I+D1D2)−1D1C2−B1C2]X2+[B1−B1D2(I+D1D2)−1D1]u(3)\begin{align} \dot{X_1 }&=A_1 X_1+B_1(u-Y_2)\\ =&A_1 X_1+B_1u-B_1\{D_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}C_1X_1+[C_2-D_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1C_2]X_2+\\ &D_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1u\} \\ =&[A_1-B_1D_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}C_1]X_1+[B_1D_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1C_2-B_1C_2]X_2+\\ &[B_1-B_1D_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1]u \end{align}\tag{3} X1​˙​==​=A1​X1​+B1​(u−Y2​)A1​X1​+B1​u−B1​{D2​(I+D1​D2​)−1C1​X1​+[C2​−D2​(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​C2​]X2​+D2​(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​u}[A1​−B1​D2​(I+D1​D2​)−1C1​]X1​+[B1​D2​(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​C2​−B1​C2​]X2​+[B1​−B1​D2​(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​]u​(3)

将(1)代入③:

X2˙=A2X2+B2Y1=A2X2+B2[(I+D1D2)−1C1X1−(I+D1D2)−1D1C2X2+(I+D1D2)−1D1u]=B2(I+D1D2)−1C1X1+[A2−B2(I+D1D2)−1D1C2]X2+B2(I+D1D2)−1D1u\begin{align} \dot{X_2 }=&A_2 X_2+B_2Y_1\\ =&A_2 X_2+B_2[(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}C_1X_1-(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1C_2X_2+(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1u]\\ =&B_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}C_1X_1+[A_2-B_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1C_2]X_2+B_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1u \tag{4} \end{align} X2​˙​===​A2​X2​+B2​Y1​A2​X2​+B2​[(I+D1​D2​)−1C1​X1​−(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​C2​X2​+(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​u]B2​(I+D1​D2​)−1C1​X1​+[A2​−B2​(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​C2​]X2​+B2​(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​u​(4)​

综上有:

A=(A1−B1D2(I+D1D2)−1C1B1D2(I+D1D2)−1D1C2−B1C2B2(I+D1D2)−1C1A2−B2(I+D1D2)−1D1C2)\mathcal{A}=\begin{pmatrix} A_1-B_1D_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}C_1 & B_1D_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1C_2-B_1C_2\\ B_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}C_1 & A_2-B_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1C_2 \end{pmatrix} A=(A1​−B1​D2​(I+D1​D2​)−1C1​B2​(I+D1​D2​)−1C1​​B1​D2​(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​C2​−B1​C2​A2​−B2​(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​C2​​)

B=(B1−B1D2(I+D1D2)−1D1B2(I+D1D2)−1D1)\mathcal{B}=\begin{pmatrix} B_1-B_1D_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1 \\ B_2(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1 \end{pmatrix} B=(B1​−B1​D2​(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​B2​(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​​)

C=((I+D1D2)−1C1−(I+D1D2)−1D1C2)\mathcal{C}=\begin{pmatrix} (I+D_1D_2)^{-1}C_1 & -(I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1C_2 \end{pmatrix} C=((I+D1​D2​)−1C1​​−(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​C2​​)

D=(I+D1D2)−1D1\mathcal{D}= (I+D_1D_2)^{-1}D_1 D=(I+D1​D2​)−1D1​
